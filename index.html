Field

    Each field also lists grants open to any discipline.

      Ruzuku combines entries checked by hand with entries found automatically. A scheduled job reads the feeds of Kenyan and international funding announcements every day and adds new calls. Entries tagged "Auto-found" have not been checked by a person: the field, deadline and amount are guessed from the announcement text.

      Always confirm eligibility, amounts and dates on the funder's own page before you start an application. Entries marked "Date not published" had no deadline in the text that was read.
